## Forks And Redistribution

- Clearly identify modified builds and who maintains them. Preserve the
  copyright, licence and modification notices required by AGPL-3.0.
- Please use a distinct name and visual identity for a fork's own branding.
  An attribution such as "Based on MK Crossfader by Mike Konstantinidis" can
  explain its origin without suggesting endorsement.
- Unmodified copies may be redistributed under AGPL-3.0 with their existing
  notices and artwork. An unchanged upstream file may be described as a copy
  of an official release; the mirror or seller is not thereby an official
  distribution channel.
- Make clear who provides support for your build or service. Do not promise
  upstream support on the maintainer's behalf.

These guidelines distinguish origin and endorsement, not commercial from
noncommercial use. They do not prohibit AGPL-compliant resale or lawful
descriptive use of the project names.

## Official Sources

To check a downloaded installer, compare its SHA-256 checksum with the one
published on the upstream release page. A checksum supplied only by a mirror
does not independently establish upstream provenance. A third party's signature
or successful notarisation alone does not make a build an official release.
